Misty Steps

Walk, cycle or roll to clear the fog from your own map.

1. Get F-Droid

Already have F-Droid or Droid-ify? Skip to step 2.

  1. On your Android phone, go to f-droid.org and tap Download F-Droid.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks if your browser may install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open F-Droid and give it a minute to load its list of apps.

2. Add my repo and install

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap the button below. F-Droid opens and asks to add the repo: tap Add.
  2. Search for Misty Steps in F-Droid and tap Install. Android asks once more, now for F-Droid: allow it the same way.

Is this safe?

Hi, I'm Tjerk. This repo is really mine, and you're welcome here. Android shows a few warnings along the way because these apps don't come from Google Play. That's normal for any app from outside the Play Store, and it doesn't mean something is wrong.

  • F-Droid is a well-known app store for free and open-source apps, run by volunteers since 2010.
  • This repo is where I publish my own apps. I build and sign every update myself. You can check it's me: my website links here.
  • The code is open on GitHub, so anyone can look at what the app does. No ads, no accounts, no tracking: your trails stay on your phone.
  • If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, that's fine: let it.

Questions or something odd? Mail me at debug@potloodgum.com.
