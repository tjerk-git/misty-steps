The whole world starts out covered in drifting mist. The only way to clear it is to go outside: wherever you walk, cycle, skate or roll, the fog lifts and the map underneath appears. Your own neighbourhood turns into a map to uncover, one step at a time.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Clear the fog:</b> press "Start wandering" and every place you pass clears in a 50 m circle, even with the screen off and the phone in your pocket.</li>
<li><b>Your trail:</b> each walk leaves a dotted line behind your character.</li>
<li><b>A little companion:</b> pick a pixel-art cat, dog, sea otter, capybara, cow or horse. They all wear hiking boots.</li>
<li><b>Your neighbourhood:</b> see how much of your neighbourhood, city, country and the world you've uncovered, and which streets you haven't walked yet.</li>
<li><b>Walk postcards:</b> after every walk, a postcard of your route to save or share.</li>
<li><b>Mist through the day:</b> the fog turns rosy at sunrise and sunset and blue-grey at night.</li>
<li><b>Own steam only:</b> walking, running, cycling, a wheelchair or mobility scooter all count. In a car or train (above 32 km/h) the fog stays put.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your trails are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. City and country borders come with the app, so working out where you are happens on your phone: nothing about where you are is sent anywhere.
