# Fun Facts Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add a "Fun facts" card with crazy comparisons to the stats page, on both apps.

**Architecture:** A pure picker over tables of real references, sentences in the strings files, a small shared text builder, and one card in the shared stats screen.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin Multiplatform shared module (kotlin.test, Compose Multiplatform resources).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-fun-facts-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No emoji anywhere.
- Ratio window: 1,5 to 9.999 times the reference (the moon line: 0,0001% to under 100%).
- Constants (all approximate, named in a comment): distance in metres: tennis_court 23.77, blue_whale 30, football_pitch 105, dom_tower 112, eiffel 330, burj 828, marathon 42195, afsluitdijk 32000, elfsteden 200000, moon 384400000 (percent); area in m2: tennis_court 261, football_pitch 7140, vatican 440000, vondelpark 470000, monaco 2020000, schiermonnikoog 40000000, texel 463000000, luxembourg 2586000000, friesland 5749000000; volume in litres: bathtub 150, garden_pool 30000, olympic_pool 2500000, ijsselmeer 5000000000000.
- Strings: `facts_title` (EN "Fun facts", NL "Weetjes") and `fact_distance_*`, `fact_area_*`, `fact_volume_*` for every reference above, each with `%1$s`.
- Work on branch `fun-facts`. Never push. Do not touch the user's phone while it is locked.

## Review Focus

- A walk with no distance, or no cleared ground: no lines, the card hidden, no "0 ..." sentence.
- Every realistic amount has a sentence: distance from 40 m to 400,000 km, area from 100 m2 to the whole country (4,2e10 m2), volume from 2e5 L to 8,4e13 L.
- The moon line: tiny shares read like "0,017%" and only inside its window.
- Dutch sentences read naturally with the number ("1,5 keer de Afsluitdijk", "590 voetbalvelden").
- The rotation is the same all day and changes between days; the card does not flicker or change while the stats page is open.
- Negative, NaN or infinite amounts: no line, no crash.
